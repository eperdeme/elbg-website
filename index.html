About

A table for every kind of player.

ELBG brings people together to play modern board games in a relaxed, friendly setting. Members bring a mix of favourites and new discoveries, and there is usually someone happy to explain the rules.

You can come along solo, bring a friend, teach something from your own collection, or simply sit down and learn something new. The aim is simple: good games, good company, and no pressure.

What We Play

Light fillers, modern classics, strategy nights and everything between.

Expect a broad mix: gateway games, co-ops, party games, Euros, card games, crunchy strategy games and the occasional campaign box. Newer players are welcome, and rules teaching is part of the culture.
