About

A table for every kind of player.

ELBG brings people together to play modern board games in a relaxed, friendly setting. Members bring a mix of old favourites and new games, and there is usually someone happy to explain the rules.

You can come along solo, bring a friend, teach something from your own collection, or simply sit down and learn something new. The aim is simple: good games, good company, and no pressure.

What We Play

Quick games, old favourites and bigger strategy nights.

Most Tuesdays there are a few tables going at once. Some people play quick card games, others settle in for something bigger, and newcomers can usually join a table and learn as they go.
